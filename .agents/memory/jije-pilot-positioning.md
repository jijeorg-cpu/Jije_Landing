name: Jíjé pilot positioning
description: Durable launch-stage and recruitment constraints for Jíjé's Toronto/GTA marketplace pilot.
---

Jíjé is preparing a Toronto/GTA pilot for an African and Afro-Caribbean food and grocery marketplace. Vendor and driver recruitment pages collect interest for review; submissions do not create marketplace accounts or mean automatic approval.

Commission rates, delivery fees, driver earnings, payout timing, launch date, and the final grocery shopper model are not finalized. Grocery shopping is exploratory.

**Why:** the user stated these product and business constraints in the recruitment brief.

**How to apply:** keep public copy and recruitment flows accurate to the prelaunch pilot; do not promise rates, earnings, payout schedules, launch timing, broad availability, or account activation.
