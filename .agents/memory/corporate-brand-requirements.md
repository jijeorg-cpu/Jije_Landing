The official mission must be used exactly wherever presented as the company mission, prominently under Our Mission on the About JÍJÉ page:

To preserve, connect and advance African and Afro-Caribbean food culture by building the digital and intelligence infrastructure that strengthens cultural preservation, expands access to its foods, knowledge and traditions, and makes them more understandable to technology.

Approved brand hierarchy:
- JÍJÉ Group — Rooted in Culture. Powered by Intelligence.
- JÍJÉ Marketplace — Your Culture, Delivered.
- JÍJÉ Intelligence — Cultural Knowledge. Real-World Intelligence.

These taglines identify the brands; they are not mandatory repeated visible
headings. The user subsequently approved removing the Marketplace hero slogan
and repeated corporate/card/footer slogans while retaining the brand hierarchy.

JÍJÉ, operated by JÍJÉ Group Inc., is the parent company and broader ecosystem.
Marketplace is its first product, not the whole company. Intelligence is the
developing technology and intelligence business focused on African and
Afro-Caribbean food knowledge, cultural data, AI, contextual understanding, and
future research and enterprise applications. Do not imply planned integrations
or AI products are already publicly available.

Preserve Partnerships/Contact inquiry destinations and functionality. The user
subsequently requested shorter, non-duplicated corporate copy on those pages.

Corporate restructuring is an enhancement, not a rebuild. Preserve all existing and unpublished functionality, including recruitment cards and their complete submission workflows, Join the Pilot, and Partnership Inquiry. Recruitment remains associated with Marketplace and may appear on its page with links elsewhere. The working project, not just the deployed site, is the source of truth.

Before implementation, inspect pending changes and relevant branches and establish a recoverable checkpoint. Verify the complete submission workflow before moving or modifying forms. After implementation, verify vendor and driver submissions using test data without affecting production records, plus desktop/mobile functionality. Show a preview for review; do not deploy automatically.

**Why:** the user explicitly supplied these requirements and official wording in the corporate restructuring clarification.

**How to apply:** retain these constraints when scoping and implementing corporate pages, navigation, mission copy, and any relocation of Marketplace recruitment.

## Brand-guide precedence

Use the supplied brand guide for corporate-page and shared-shell visual standards, while retaining the newer
approved corporate mission, three taglines, and Toronto/GTA prelaunch positioning.
Do not import the guide's older marketplace-only mission or availability claims.
Approved legal document bodies and PDFs remain verbatim; apply website branding
in their surrounding interface rather than silently rewriting legal text.

**Why:** the guide predates the explicitly approved corporate wording, and visual
brand normalization is not approval to revise legal documents.

**How to apply:** distinguish visual guidance from current business copy whenever
the guide or other historical material conflicts with approved website wording.

## Marketplace preservation and corporate brevity

The user clarified that the updated, redesigned Marketplace is the approved
baseline—not the old whole-page design. Keep the multi-Kaya illustration,
updated typography/spacing, updated
Discover / Find your fit / Stay connected flow, updated ecosystem section,
Toronto/GTA recruitment, and “Follow the Marketplace pilot” waitlist.
Restore ONLY the original three-photograph section: “One Place for Culture &
Community,” its original supporting description, Authentic Food, Grocery

the entire business strategy. About has four main sections: Who We Are, the exact

limited the original-design restoration to the three photographic cards and
associated heading/wording. Later approved simplifications supersede the old
hero slogan and colour treatment, not the retained Marketplace structure.

**How to apply:** use the updated Marketplace as the baseline, consult the original
version only for that photographic section, and leave approved corporate content,
navigation/footer, recruitment cards and form/backend functionality untouched.

them to avoid duplicated navigation and content.

keep old partnership addresses working through redirects rather than recreating

## Approved visual simplification

Use white general website backgrounds, a compact mobile logo, and JÍJÉ red/green
text accents instead of brown. Keep intentionally coloured green sections.
Avoid repeating slogans, location labels, process disclaimers and section
introductions. Preserve functional CTAs, required consent, legal links and the
official mission while shortening presentation.

**Why:** the user supplied detailed annotated mobile screens and approved these
changes after confirming the interpretation. Extra explanatory copy had become
repetitive rather than useful.

**How to apply:** do not restore removed slogans or cream/yellow background
washes as part of later branding work. Distinguish essential application/legal
disclosures from optional promotional wording.

The homepage's green story/community area should stay compact, with a proper
rounded button for “Read our story,” consistent with the other CTAs. Do not
reintroduce the red corporate/location eyebrow above the homepage hero.
Keep the two headings aligned in a left column and both button rows aligned
at the same starting position in a right column on desktop.

**Why:** the user explicitly approved this refinement because the green area
looked empty and the story link needed the same button treatment as the other CTAs.
The user subsequently requested matching horizontal alignment for the button rows.

**How to apply:** retain this compact presentation when refining the homepage;
do not add filler copy or expand the green area just to occupy space.
